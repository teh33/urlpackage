Why this exists

Not bookmarks. Not a silo. A file.

URLPackage is meant to sit above individual URLs and outside any one browser or app. You can attach it to a class, report, blog post, AI prompt, or research trail; someone else can open it, browse it, add to it, and export their own version.

Future .urlpackage bundles can be zip files containing manifest.json plus images and cached metadata. The plain JSON format stays the canonical, easy-to-read base.
